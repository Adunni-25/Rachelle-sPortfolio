About Me

I'm Rachelle, a Computer Science student at the University of Lagos with a deep curiosity for cybersecurity and artificial intelligence. I graduated as Valedictorian of my set — Best Overall Student across all three years of senior secondary school — and held the highest JAMB score in my school's history. But the achievement I'm most proud of is returning in my first university holiday to mentor the students who came after me, one of whom went on to break my record. That moment told me something important about the kind of person I want to be in tech — someone who builds others up. I'm the founder of Simply Aurora, a growing vision for a tech brand and community for women in tech, and I'm motivated every day by faith, curiosity, and the belief that the best is still ahead.
